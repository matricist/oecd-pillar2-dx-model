### Jurisdictional ETR and Top-up Tax Rate

#### ETR

Entity-level GloBE Income or Loss data and adjustments are represented by

$$
\text{Globe-income-data-entt}
\in
\mathbb{R}^{N\times K_G}.
$$

Each column represents an Entity-level amount or adjustment required in determining GloBE Income or Loss.

Similarly, Entity-level Adjusted Covered Tax data and adjustments are represented by

$$
\text{Covered-tax-data-entt}
\in
\mathbb{R}^{N\times K_T}.
$$

Each adjustment column is represented as a signed amount. A positive amount increases the relevant result and a negative amount decreases it.

For example,

$$
\text{Covered-tax-data-entt}
=
[
\text{NetTaxesExpense},
\ldots
].
$$

The Entity-level data is aggregated to the Full Calculation Groups defined by $\text{Entt-group}$:

$$
\text{Globe-income-data-group}
:=
\text{Entt-group}^{T}
\text{Globe-income-data-entt}
\in
\mathbb{R}^{M_{Calc}\times K_G}
$$

and

$$
\text{Covered-tax-data-group}
:=
\text{Entt-group}^{T}
\text{Covered-tax-data-entt}
\in
\mathbb{R}^{M_{Calc}\times K_T}.
$$

Certain adjustments are determined only after Entity-level amounts have been aggregated to the Full Calculation Group. Such adjustments are represented separately by

$$
\text{Globe-income-adjustment-group}
\in
\mathbb{R}^{M_{Calc}\times L_G}
$$

and

$$
\text{Covered-tax-adjustment-group}
\in
\mathbb{R}^{M_{Calc}\times L_T}.
$$

Accordingly, jurisdictional GloBE Income or Loss is

$$
\boxed{
\text{globe-income-loss-group}
=
\text{Globe-income-data-group}\mathbf{1}_{K_G}
+
\text{Globe-income-adjustment-group}\mathbf{1}_{L_G}
}
$$

and jurisdictional Adjusted Covered Taxes are

$$
\boxed{
\text{act-group}
=
\text{Covered-tax-data-group}\mathbf{1}_{K_T}
+
\text{Covered-tax-adjustment-group}\mathbf{1}_{L_T}
}
$$

where

$$
\text{globe-income-loss-group},
\text{act-group}
\in
\mathbb{R}^{M_{Calc}}.
$$

Define the positive GloBE Income indicator vector as

$$
\text{positive-income}
:=
\mathbf{1}_{\{\text{globe-income-loss-group}>0\}}
\in
\{0,1\}^{M_{Calc}}.
$$

The ETR is defined only for Full Calculation Groups with positive GloBE Income.

For each Full Calculation Group $j$,

$$
\text{etr}_j
:=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\text{act-group}_j}
{\text{globe-income-loss-group}_j},
&
\text{if }\text{globe-income-loss-group}_j>0,
\\[8pt]
\text{undefined},
&
\text{if }\text{globe-income-loss-group}_j\le0.
\end{cases}
$$

For implementation purposes, division is performed only on

$$
\text{pos}
:=
\{j\mid \text{globe-income-loss-group}_j>0\}.
$$

Thus,

$$
\boxed{
\text{etr}_{\text{pos}}
=
\text{act-group}_{\text{pos}}
\oslash
\text{globe-income-loss-group}_{\text{pos}}
}
$$

The ETR calculation therefore returns

$$
(\text{etr},\text{positive-income}),
$$

so that downstream calculations can distinguish a valid ETR from a Full Calculation Group for which the ETR is not defined.

구체적인 Net Globe Income 및 Adjusted Covered Taxes 계산 방법은 Chapter 3에서 다루도록 한다.

#### Top-up Tax Rate
The Top-up Tax Rate is calculated only for Full Calculation Groups with positive Net GloBE Income.  
Negative Acjusted Covered Taxes may otherwise cause the calculated Top-up Tax Rate to exceed the Minimum Rate.

Accordingly, define the Excess Negative Tax Expense generated in the current Fiscal Year as

$$
\boxed{
    \text{ente-currnet} := [-\text{act-group}]^{+} \odot \text{positive-income}
}
$$
where the positive-part operator is applied element-wise.

Where the Excess Negative Tax Expense ("ENTE") administrative procedure applies, the negative portion of Adjusted Covered Taxes is excluded from the current-year ETR computation. Therefore, define Adjusted Covered Taxes after the current-year ENTE adjustment as

$$
\boxed{
    \text{act-after-ente-current} := [\text{act-group}]^{+}
}
$$

for Full Calculation Groups with positive Net GloBE Income.

The ETR used in determining the Top-up Tax Rate is therefore

$$
\text{etr}_{\text{pos}}^{*} = \text{act-after-ente-current}_{\text{pos}} \oslash \text{globe-income-loss-group}_{\text{pos}}
$$

Accordingly,
$$\boxed{
\text{tt-percent}_{\text{pos}} := [0.15 \cdot \mathbf{1}_{\mid \text{pos} \mid} - \text{etr}_{\mid \text{pos} \mid}^{*}]^{+}}
$$

For the remaining Full Calculation Groups,

$$
\text{tt-percent}_{\overline{\text{pos}}} := \mathbb{0}_{|\overline{\text{pos}|}}
$$

Thus,

$$
\text{tt-percent} \in [0,0.15]^{M_{Calc}}
$$

The current-year ENTE is carried forward and reduces Adjusted Covered Taxes in subsequent Fiscal Years in which the relevant Full Calculation Group has positive Net GloBE Income and positive Adjusted Covered Taxes, subject to the remaining carry-forward balance.

### Jurisdictional Top-up Tax and its Allocation

먼저 Top-up Tax Rate ("TT Rate"), Excess Profit, Additional Current Top-up Tax ("ACTT") 및 QDMTT 금액이 계산되었음을 전제로 먼저 전개하겠다.

Jurisdictional Top-up Tax Percentage를 나타내는 vector가 있다고 가정하자. $\text{tt-percent} \in [0,0.15]^{M_{Calc}}$  
Jurisdictional ACT를 나타내는 vector가 있다고 가정하자.
$\text{act-jur} \in \mathbb{R}^{M_{Calc}}$  
Entity의 ACT를 나타내는 vector가 있다고 가정하자.
$\text{act-entt} \in \mathbb{R}^{N}$    
Jurisdictional Excess Profit 금액을 나타내는 real vector가 있다고 가정하자.
$\text{excess-profit-jur} \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^{M_{Calc}}$    
Entity Excess Profit 금액을 나타내는 real vector가 있다고 가정하자.
$\text{excess-profit-entt} \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^{N}$    
Jurisdictional QDMTT 금액을 나타내는 real vector가 있다고 가정하자.
$\text{qdmtt} \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}^{M_{Calc}}$    
그리고 각 기업의 GloBE Income 금액을 나타내는 real vector가 있다고 가정하자.
$\text{globe-income} \in \mathbb{R}^{N}$    
$\text{globe-income}$이 양수인 경우만을 남겨둔 금액을 나타내는 real vector를 다음과 같이 계산하자. $\text{pos-globe-income} = [\text{globe-income}]^{+}$
그리고 Top-up Tax Calculation에 사용되는 Group을 Group Hash Code를 기준으로 $\text{Entt-group} \in \{0,1\}^{N \times M_{Calc}}$

$(\text{Entt-group})_ij=1$ iff Entity $i$ belongs to Full Calc Group $j$.

The following cases are mutually exclusive:
1. the QDMTT Safe Harbour applies;
2. the QDMTT Safe Harbour does not apply, Article 4.1.5 applies, and the Excess Negative Tax Expense administrative procedure is applied for the Fiscal Year; or
3. the QDMTT Safe Harbour does not apply, Article 4.1.5 applies, and the Excess Negative Tax Expense administrative procedure is not applied for the Fiscal Year; or
4. neither of the above applies.

그리고 indicator 정의를:
$$
state^{qsh},\;
state^{ente}, \;
state^{4.1.5},\;
state^{normal}
\in\{0,1\}^{M_{Calc}}
$$

where
$$
state^{qsh}_j=1
$$
iff the QDMTT Safe Harbour applies to Full Calculation Group $j$;

where
$$
state^{ente}_j=1
$$
iff: 
1. the QDMTT Safe Harbour does not apply;
2. Article 4.1.5 applies; and
3. the Excess Negative Tax Expense administrative procedure is applied for the Fiscal Year;

$$
state^{4.1.5}_j=1
$$
iff:
1. the QDMTT Safe Harbour does not apply;
2. Article 4.1.5 applies; and
3. the Excess Negative Tax Expense administrative procedure is not applied for the Fiscal Year;

and
$$
state^{normal}_j=1
$$
iff neither of the above applies.

세 상태는 mutually exclusive이므로 다음이 성립한다.
$$
state^{qsh} + state^{ente} + state^{4.1.5} + state^{normal} = \mathbf{1}_{M_{Calc}}
$$

Expected Adjusted Covered Taxes Amount $\text{exp-act} \in \mathbb{R}^{M_{Calc}}$를 다음과 같이 정의하자.

$$
\text{expected-act-entt} := 0.15 \cdot \text{globe-income}
$$

$$
\text{expected-act-group} := 0.15 \cdot \text{globe-income-loss-group}
$$

$$
\text{art-4.1.5-eligible} := \mathbf{1}_{\{\text{act-entt}<0\}} \odot \mathbf{1}_{\{\text{act-entt}<\text{expected-act-entt}\}}
$$

$$
\text{art-4.1.5-basis-entt} := \text{art-4.1.5-eligible} \odot (\text{expected-act-entt} - \text{act-entt})
$$


$$
\text{art-4.1.5-basis-group} := \text{Entt-group}^{T} \text{art-4.1.5-basis-entt} \in \mathbb{R}^{M_{Calc}}
$$

$$
\text{group-pos-income} = \text{Entt-group}^{T} \text{pos-globe-income}
$$

$\text{actt-prior-entt}$는 사전에 주어지는 것으로 간주한다.     
각 state마다 $\text{tt-jur}$를 계산하면 다음과 같다.

$$\text{tt-group}^{qsh} = \text{tt-group}^{ente} = \mathbf{0}_{M_{Calc}}$$
$$\text{tt-group}^{4.1.5} = [\text{expected-act-group} - \text{act-group} - \text{qdmtt}]^{+}$$
$$
\text{tt-group}^{normal} = [\text{tt-percent} \odot \text{excess-profit-jur} + \text{actt-current-jur} + \text{Entt-group}^{T} \text{actt-prior-entt} - \text{qdmtt-jur-normal}]^{+}
$$
$$
\text{tt-group}^{\text{normal-less-actt-prior}} = \text{tt-percent} \cdot \text{excess-profit-jur} + \text{actt-current-jur} - \text{qdmtt-jur-normal}
$$


$$
\text{tt-group} = state^{qsh} \odot \text{tt-group}^{qsh} + state^{ente} \odot \text{tt-group}^{ente} + state^{4.1.5} \odot \text{tt-group}^{4.1.5} + state^{normal} \odot \text{tt-group}^{normal}
$$

$$
\text{Tt-alloc}^{4.1.5} = \operatorname{diag}(\text{art-4.1.5-basis-entt})\text{Entt-group} \cdot \operatorname{diag}(\text{art-4.1.5-basis-group})^{\dagger} \in [0,1]^{N \times M_{Calc}}
$$

$$
\text{Tt-alloc}^{normal} = \operatorname{diag}(\text{pos-globe-income})\text{Entt-group} \cdot \operatorname{diag}(\text{group-pos-income})^{\dagger} \in [0,1]^{N, M_{Calc}}
$$

$$
\text{tt-entt} = \text{Tt-alloc}^{4.1.5} \operatorname{diag}(state^{4.1.5})\text{tt-group}^{4.1.5} + \text{Tt-alloc}^{normal} \operatorname{diag}(state^{normal})\text{tt-group}^{\text{normal-less-actt-prior}} + \text{state}_{entt}^{normal}(\text{actt-prior-entt} - \text{qdmtt-prior-entt})
$$
