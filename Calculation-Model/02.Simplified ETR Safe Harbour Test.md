## Exports

### Simplified ETR Safe Harbour Function

This document defines and exports `calculate_simplified_etr_safe_harbour`.

- **Input:** Tested-Jurisdiction membership, financial accounting amounts, signed income and tax adjustments, elections, prior-year eligibility information, and the jurisdictional taxing-rights results.
- **Output:** Jurisdictional Profit or Loss before Income Tax ("JPBT"), Jurisdictional Income Tax Expense ("JITE"), Simplified Income or Loss, Simplified Taxes, Simplified ETR, the Simplified Adjustment for Negative Taxes, and the final Safe Harbour result.
- **Relation output:** One calculated row is produced in [`Relations.JurisdictionsSetr`](<Relations.md#jurisdictionssetr>) for each Tested Jurisdiction.

# Validation

## Structural Validation

- All Entity-level inputs must use the same Entity ordering and fiscal year.
- Each Entity included in the calculation must belong to exactly one Tested Jurisdiction before the Same-country Investment Entity election is applied.
- Each `SetrAdjustments` and `SetrElections` row must identify exactly one Tested Jurisdiction.
- All monetary values and adjustment amounts must be finite.
- Signed adjustments must follow the convention that a positive value increases and a negative value decreases the relevant amount.

## Domain Validation

- Excluded Entities are omitted unless an Article 1.5.3 election applies.
- The financial accounting data must be the data used to prepare the MNE Group's Consolidated Financial Statements consistently with Articles 3.1.2 and 3.1.3.
- If a QDMTT jurisdiction requires its Local Financial Accounting Standard ("LFAS"), that standard must also be used for this Safe Harbour unless the jurisdiction permits the CFS accounting standard. A CFS-standard election offered by QDMTT LFAS jurisdictions must be applied consistently across all jurisdictions offering that election and in subsequent Fiscal Years.
- Adjustments must satisfy the matching, full allocation, single expense and loss, and single tax principles described below.
- A Five-Year Election or other continuing election must be applied consistently throughout its required period, including Fiscal Years in which this Safe Harbour is not applied.

## Runtime Validation

Raise a **Runtime Validation Error** when:

- a Tested Jurisdiction contains an Entity that belongs to another Tested Jurisdiction;
- a required adjustment is unavailable or cannot be allocated reliably;
- the accounted tax rate needed to recast deferred tax expense is zero or unavailable;
- the first-election or re-entry requirement is not supported by complete data for every Fiscal Year beginning in the relevant preceding 24-month period; or
- an early-application result is requested without complete implementation and election data for every jurisdiction having taxing rights over the Tested Jurisdiction.

# Operation

## Notation

Bold uppercase symbols denote matrices, bold lowercase names denote column vectors, and individual entries are scalars. The calculations below operate on $K$ Tested Jurisdictions formed from $N$ Entities.

| Symbol | Type and meaning |
|---|---|
| $\mathcal{V}=\{1,\ldots,N\}$ | Set of Entity indices. |
| $\mathcal{T}=\{1,\ldots,K\}$ | Set of Tested Jurisdiction indices. |
| $\mathbf{G}_{SETR}\in\{0,1\}^{K\times N}$ | Tested-Jurisdiction membership matrix. |
| $\mathbf{fanil}\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times1}$ | Entity-level Financial Accounting Net Income or Loss. |
| $\mathbf{current\text{-}tax}\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times1}$ | Current income tax expense or benefit accrued in FANIL. |
| $\mathbf{deferred\text{-}tax}\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times1}$ | Deferred income tax expense or benefit accrued in FANIL. |
| $\mathbf{consolidated\text{-}deferred\text{-}tax}\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times1}$ | Consolidated-level deferred tax attributable to each Entity. |
| $\mathbf{A}^{I}\in\mathbb{R}^{K\times8}$ | Signed Simplified Income adjustment matrix. |
| $\mathbf{A}^{T}\in\mathbb{R}^{K\times8}$ | Signed Simplified Taxes adjustment matrix. |
| $\mathbf{JPBT},\mathbf{JITE},\mathbf{SI},\mathbf{ST}\in\mathbb{R}^{K\times1}$ | JPBT, JITE, Simplified Income or Loss, and Simplified Taxes. |
| $\mathbf{setr}\in\mathbb{R}^{K\times1}$ | Simplified ETR; undefined where Simplified Income is not positive. |
| $\mathbf{elected},\mathbf{eligible},\mathbf{passed}\in\{0,1\}^{K\times1}$ | Election, eligibility, and test-result indicators. |
| $r_{min}=0.15$ | GloBE Minimum Rate. |

## Constructing Tested Jurisdictions

A Tested Jurisdiction contains the Entities located in one jurisdiction for which the GloBE Rules require a separate ETR calculation. Apply the following separation rules:

1. ordinary CEs located in the same jurisdiction form one Tested Jurisdiction;
2. members of the same JV Group located in the same jurisdiction form one Tested Jurisdiction;
3. each stand-alone JV forms a separate Tested Jurisdiction;
4. members of the same MOSG located in the same jurisdiction form one Tested Jurisdiction;
5. each Single MOCE forms a separate Tested Jurisdiction;
6. each eligible Investment Entity or Insurance Investment Entity forms a separate Tested Jurisdiction; and
7. each eligible Stateless CE forms a separate Tested Jurisdiction.

The separation priority is Stateless Entity, Investment Entity, MOSG or Single MOCE, JV Group, and ordinary CE. This prevents a narrower calculation group from being blended into a broader group.

At the Filing CE's Annual Election, Same-country Investment Entities may instead be combined with ordinary CEs located in the same jurisdiction. This applies only where:

- the ordinary CEs are not MOCEs;
- all CE-owners of each Investment Entity are located in the same jurisdiction as the Investment Entity; and
- none of those owners has made an Article 7.5 or Article 7.6 election.

For Tested Jurisdiction $l$ and Entity $j$:

$$
(\mathbf{G}_{SETR})_{lj}=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{Entity }j\text{ belongs to Tested Jurisdiction }l.
$$

Every included Entity must satisfy

$$
\sum_{l\in\mathcal{T}}(\mathbf{G}_{SETR})_{lj}=1.
$$

## Source of Information

The OECD permits reliable jurisdictional data where it produces the same result as Entity-level aggregation. This implementation uses `CompaniesSetr` and $\mathbf{G}_{SETR}$; an ingestion process using direct jurisdictional data must populate the same four jurisdictional base vectors before invoking the exported calculation. Consolidation eliminations for intragroup transactions are reversed unless an Article 3.2.8 election applies to transactions within the same Tested Jurisdiction. Purchase price accounting effects are also reversed unless the M&A Simplification applies.

The jurisdictional base amounts are

$$
\mathbf{FANIL}^{TJ}:=\mathbf{G}_{SETR}\mathbf{fanil},
$$

$$
\mathbf{CurrentTax}^{TJ}:=\mathbf{G}_{SETR}\mathbf{current\text{-}tax},
$$

$$
\mathbf{DeferredTaxInFANIL}^{TJ}
:=\mathbf{G}_{SETR}\mathbf{deferred\text{-}tax},
$$

$$
\mathbf{ConsolidatedDeferredTax}^{TJ}
:=\mathbf{G}_{SETR}\mathbf{consolidated\text{-}deferred\text{-}tax}.
$$

The Jurisdictional Income Tax Expense and JPBT before their respective adjustments are

$$
\mathbf{JITE}_0
:=
\mathbf{CurrentTax}^{TJ}
+\mathbf{DeferredTaxInFANIL}^{TJ}
+\mathbf{ConsolidatedDeferredTax}^{TJ},
$$

$$
\mathbf{JPBT}_0
:=
\mathbf{FANIL}^{TJ}
+\mathbf{CurrentTax}^{TJ}
+\mathbf{DeferredTaxInFANIL}^{TJ}.
$$

Consolidated-level deferred tax is included in JITE but not added back in JPBT because it was not accrued in FANIL.

An elected Covered Tax expense not presented as income tax expense is added both to JPBT and Simplified Taxes through the optional adjustment columns so that the associated expense is reversed from income and the tax enters the numerator.

## Simplified Income or Loss

The columns of $\mathbf{A}^{I}$ are, in order:

1. `basic_income_adjustment`;
2. `industry_income_adjustment`;
3. `conditional_income_adjustment`;
4. `optional_income_adjustment`;
5. `after_year_end_income_adjustment`;
6. `cross_border_income_adjustment`;
7. `tax_neutral_income_adjustment`; and
8. `integrity_income_adjustment`.

The Basic Adjustments must remove Excluded Dividends and Excluded Equity Gains or Losses and add back bribes, kickbacks, other illegal payments, and each fine or penalty of at least EUR 250,000.

Industry Adjustments apply Article 3.2.9 to insurance-company policyholder-tax recharges unless the Annual Election not to apply it is made, apply Article 3.2.10 to Additional Tier One and Restricted Tier One capital, and exclude International Shipping Income and Qualified Ancillary International Shipping Income unless the applicable Five-Year Election permits their inclusion for a Fiscal Year with positive aggregate qualifying shipping income.

Conditional Adjustments cover:

- positive Included Revaluation Method Gains or Prior Period Errors and Changes in Accounting Principle reported in equity or OCI, unless the item is taxed at least at the Minimum Rate and the associated tax is also reported in equity or OCI; a related DTL must additionally be a Recapture Exception Accrual;
- Chapter 6 adjustments for GloBE-to-book differences arising from an M&A Transaction;
- the M&A Simplification where every relevant non-goodwill asset and liability retains the same tax basis and the corresponding deferred tax is accrued at no less than the Minimum Rate;
- goodwill impairment or amortisation without a corresponding DTL at no less than the Minimum Rate; and
- amounts allocated to the Fiscal Year under an Article 6.3.4 election.

Optional Income Adjustments include permitted Chapter 3 elections, the NMCE Simplified Calculation, elected QRTC or MTTC treatment, and the elected Substance-based Tax Incentive adjustment. Asymmetric Foreign Exchange Currency Gains or Losses and accrued pension expense are adjusted by default, but each adjustment may be switched off by its own Five-Year Election.

After-year-end, cross-border, tax-neutral, and integrity adjustments follow the rules described in their corresponding sections below.

The Simplified Income or Loss is

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf{SI}
:=
\mathbf{JPBT}_0+\mathbf{A}^{I}\mathbf{1}_8
}.
$$

For Tested Jurisdiction $l$, $SI_l\le0$ is treated as a Simplified Loss for the test.

## Simplified Taxes

The columns of $\mathbf{A}^{T}$ are, in order:

1. `policy_tax_adjustment`;
2. `correlation_tax_adjustment`;
3. `uncertain_and_unpaid_tax_adjustment`;
4. `deferred_tax_adjustment`;
5. `optional_tax_adjustment`;
6. `after_year_end_tax_adjustment`;
7. `cross_border_tax_adjustment`; and
8. `tax_neutral_and_integrity_tax_adjustment`.

The mandatory tax adjustments must:

- remove taxes that are not Covered Taxes and apply the required treatment to refunds and credits;
- remove current and deferred tax expense related to income excluded from Simplified Income;
- remove uncertain tax accruals and Disallowed Accruals until paid;
- remove current tax expense not expected to be paid within three years;
- disregard valuation allowances and accounting recognition adjustments affecting DTAs;
- remove deferred tax effects caused by a change in tax rate;
- remove deferred tax expense from the generation and use of tax credits, except for a permitted Substitute Loss Carry-forward DTA; and
- remove deferred tax expense attributable to a DTL that is not a Recapture Exception Accrual.

The last rule does not exclude the reversal of a DTL arising from an M&A Transaction where the M&A Simplification includes the related book effect, or the reversal of a DTL previously included in an ETR computation under a Qualified IIR, Qualified UTPR, or QDMTT.

After the exclusions above, net deferred tax expense is recast at the Minimum Rate when the accounted tax rate exceeds that rate. For a Tested Jurisdiction subject to one Covered Tax rate and tax base:

$$
\text{RecastDeferredTax}_l
=
\text{EligibleDeferredTax}_l
\frac{r_{min}}{\text{AccountedTaxRate}_l}.
$$

No upward recast is made where the accounted tax rate is at or below the Minimum Rate. Where multiple Covered Taxes use different rates or tax bases, the amount must instead be prepared using a method consistent with the deferred-tax recasting principles of the GloBE Rules.

Optional Tax Adjustments include elected Covered Taxes expensed outside income tax expense, taxes on included equity-reported income, elected QRTC or MTTC treatment, the Substance-based Tax Incentive Safe Harbour, and the GloBE Loss Election.

Before applying the negative-tax carry-forward rules, Simplified Taxes are

$$
\mathbf{ST}^{pre}
:=
\mathbf{JITE}_0+\mathbf{A}^{T}\mathbf{1}_8.
$$

Any opening Simplified Adjustment for Negative Taxes or Excess Negative Tax Carry-forward applied in the current Fiscal Year reduces Simplified Taxes:

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf{ST}
:=
\mathbf{ST}^{pre}-\mathbf{negative\text{-}tax\text{-}cf\text{-}applied}
}.
$$

## Simplified Loss and Negative Taxes

If $SI_l\le0$ and $ST_l<0$, the signed Simplified Adjustment for Negative Taxes is

$$
\text{NegativeTaxAdjustment}_l
:=
\min\left(0,ST_l-r_{min}SI_l\right).
$$

Its positive carry-forward amount is

$$
\text{NegativeTaxCarryForwardGenerated}_l
:=
-\text{NegativeTaxAdjustment}_l.
$$

It is carried forward and later reduces Simplified Taxes or Adjusted Covered Taxes in the same manner as an Excess Negative Tax Carry-forward.

During the five-Fiscal-Year Transitional Period beginning with the Transition Year, an eligible MNE Group may elect the Loss DTA Adjustment instead. That method is available only while the Tested Jurisdiction has a tax loss, Simplified Loss, and Net Negative Taxes in the Transition Year and every preceding year of that period. The adjustment comprises the Minimum-Rate-recast loss DTA attributable to permanent differences plus Minimum-Rate-recast DTLs attributable to goodwill and other indefinite-life intangibles. It reduces subsequent loss-DTA reversals, but not below zero.

## After-Year-End and Cross-Border Adjustments

By default, a later change to Covered Tax liability or income is included in the accrual year. A Five-Year Election may instead assign changes accrued within 12 months after year-end to the transaction year across all jurisdictions; transfer-pricing adjustments use their separate election.

A net decrease in prior-year Covered Tax accrued after 12 months may be excluded from the accrual year if inclusion would reduce the current Simplified ETR below the Minimum Rate and the recomputed prior-year Simplified ETR or GloBE ETR remains at least the Minimum Rate after all corresponding adjustments.

Income and taxes of PEs and Flow-through Entities follow Articles 3.4, 3.5, and 4.3.2(b). Other cross-border taxes described in Article 4.3.2(a), (c), (d), and (e) are excluded from all Tested Jurisdictions by default, except source-jurisdiction withholding tax on a distribution. A Five-Year Election may allocate those taxes under Articles 4.3.2 and 4.3.3 where the receiving jurisdiction does not have a QDMTT.

The PE Simplification Election and transfer-pricing elections described by the OECD are represented by signed cross-border income and tax adjustments. These adjustments must preserve the amounts, timing, foreign-tax-credit limits, and corresponding counterparty adjustments required by those rules.

## Tax-Neutral Entities and Integrity Adjustments

Apply Articles 7.1 and 7.2 to a tax-neutral UPE. Its Simplified Income and Simplified Taxes may be set to zero when all ownership is held by the applicable Qualified Persons and, for a Deductible Dividend Regime, all income is distributed as Deductible Dividends.

The Simplified Income and Simplified Taxes of a non-UPE Tax Transparent Entity are set to zero when all its income and taxes are allocated to PEs or CE-owners under Articles 3.5 and 4.3.2. The Top-up Tax of an Investment Entity or Insurance Investment Entity is deemed zero when it is tax transparent for all CE-owners under an Article 7.5 election or the Article 10.2 definition.

The integrity adjustments must enforce:

1. **Matching principle:** intragroup income is not recognised later than the corresponding expense and the amounts match. A mismatch solely caused by one party's required use of LFAS is disregarded.
2. **Full allocation principle:** all profit or loss is allocated to a Tested Jurisdiction.
3. **Single expense and loss principle:** each expense or loss is deducted once in one Tested Jurisdiction.
4. **Single tax principle:** each tax is recorded once in one Tested Jurisdiction.

Duplicate expenses, losses, and taxes are excluded from every affected jurisdiction. An unmatched expense is removed from the jurisdiction recording the expense, and unallocated income is included in the jurisdiction required by the GloBE Rules. An intragroup financial instrument must be classified consistently by issuer and holder using the issuer's accounting standard.

## Eligibility and Applicability Date

A Tested Jurisdiction is ineligible if it consists of:

- a Stateless CE, unless the tax-transparent-entity rule above deems all its income and taxes zero;
- an Investment Entity, unless its Top-up Tax is deemed zero under the tax-transparency rule above; or
- CEs subject to an Article 7.3 Eligible Distribution Tax System election while a Deemed Distribution Tax Recapture Account balance remains outstanding at the beginning of the Fiscal Year.

For a first election, the Tested Jurisdiction must have had no Top-up Tax liability in every Fiscal Year beginning during the 24 months before the first day of the election year. A year in which the group was outside the GloBE Rules or QDMTT counts as having no Top-up Tax liability. Qualification under the Transitional CbCR Safe Harbour also satisfies the no-liability condition for first entry.

After this Safe Harbour has been elected, it may ordinarily be elected again in the immediately following Fiscal Year. If it is not elected in a later year, re-entry requires no Top-up Tax liability in each Fiscal Year beginning during the relevant preceding 24-month period under either the full GloBE Rules or a Specified Safe Harbour. The Transitional CbCR Safe Harbour and QDMTT Safe Harbour are not Specified Safe Harbours for this purpose. Additional Current Top-up Tax attributable to an earlier Fiscal Year is ignored for the re-entry test.

The general availability condition is

$$
\text{GeneralAvailable}
:=
\mathbf{1}_{\{\text{FiscalYearStart}\ge\text{31 December 2026}\}}.
$$

For a Fiscal Year beginning on or after 31 December 2025 but before 31 December 2026, an implementing jurisdiction may adopt the optional early rule. For Tested Jurisdiction $l$, let $\mathbf{r}^{(l)}=\mathbf{taxing\text{-}right\text{-}jur}^{(l)}$, let $\mathbf{a}$ be derived from `JurisdictionImplementations.has_early_setr`, and let $\mathbf{q}^{(l)}$ be derived from `SetrEarlyElections.elected` under the legislation of each taxing-right jurisdiction. Then

$$
\text{AllRightsEarly}_l
:=
\left(\mathbf{1}_M^{T}\mathbf{r}^{(l)}>0\right)
\land
\left[
\mathbf{r}^{(l)}le\mathbf{a}\odot\mathbf{q}^{(l)}
\right],
$$

$$
\text{EarlyCondition}_l
:=
\text{QdmttSafeHarbourApplies}_l
\lor
\left(\mathbf{1}_M^{T}\mathbf{r}^{(l)}=1\right)
\lor
\text{AllRightsEarly}_l.
$$

Thus,

$$
\text{DateAvailable}_l
:=
\text{GeneralAvailable}_l
\lor
\left(
\mathbf{1}_{\{\text{FiscalYearStart}\ge\text{31 December 2025}\}}
\land\text{EarlyCondition}_l
\right).
$$

The complete eligibility result is

$$
\mathbf{eligible}
:=
\mathbf{date\text{-}available}
\odot\mathbf{tested\text{-}jurisdiction\text{-}eligible}
\odot\mathbf{entry\text{-}or\text{-}reentry\text{-}eligible}
\odot\mathbf{integrity\text{-}requirements\text{-}met}.
$$

## Safe Harbour Test

For positive Simplified Income:

$$
\text{setr}_l:=\frac{ST_l}{SI_l}.
$$

The test is satisfied when the Tested Jurisdiction has a Simplified Loss, its Simplified ETR is at least the Minimum Rate, or the applicable tax-neutral rule deems its Top-up Tax to be zero:

$$
\mathbf{passed}
:=
\mathbf{1}_{\{\mathbf{SI}\le0\}}
\lor
\left(
\mathbf{1}_{\{\mathbf{SI}>0\}}
\odot\mathbf{1}_{\{\mathbf{setr}\ge r_{min}\}}
\right)
\lor
\mathbf{tax\text{-}neutral\text{-}deemed\text{-}zero}.
$$

At the Filing CE's Annual Election, the Safe Harbour applies where both eligibility and the test are satisfied:

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf{applied}
:=
\mathbf{elected}\odot\mathbf{eligible}\odot\mathbf{passed}
}.
$$

Where $\text{applied}_l=1$, the Top-up Tax for Tested Jurisdiction $l$ is deemed to be zero for the Fiscal Year. A Tested Jurisdiction that does not satisfy these conditions proceeds to the full GloBE calculation.
